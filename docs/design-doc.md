# Magentaprint

A NeoForge 1.21.1 mod that adds pink/indigo/purple-and-black styled “creative” utility items (similar to Create's Creative Motor) to Vanilla Minecraft.

None of these items have survival crafting recipes by default. They are designed for mapmakers, server admins, and modpack developers. Modpack creators are free to add custom recipes\!

## Features

> **Note:** All creative tools, weapons, and armor have infinite durability and cannot be broken.

### Combat and Gear

* **Creative Armor:** Each piece reduces incoming damage by 25%. Wearing the full set cancels all incoming damage events entirely.
* **Creative Sword:** Deals infinite damage with no attack cooldown.
* **Creative Mace:** Instantly deletes any entity hit with it.
* **Creative Bow:** Requires no ammunition, and can be configured to fire alternate items (like fireworks).
* **Creative Crossbow:** Charges instantly, shares the Creative Bow's infinite ammo features.
* **Creative Shield:** Immune to axe disabled states and reflects incoming damage back at attackers.
* **Creative Elytra:** Serves as a flying alternative to the Creative Chestplate.
* **Creative Wolf Armor:** Renders wolves entirely immune to damage.
* **Creative Horse Armor:** Renders horses entirely immune to damage.

### Tools and Utility

* **Creative Tools:** Instantly mine any block (Infinite Mining Tier).
* **Creative Hoe:** Provides inherent Fortune X (+10 Fortune level) on harvest.
* **Creative Shears:** Functions as a universal tool (Axes, Pickaxes, Shovels, etc. combined).
* **Creative Fishing Rod:** Instant bites with guaranteed highest-tier loot drops. Never breaks and pulls entities with 5× normal launch force.
* **Creative Sponge:** Instantly clears all fluids in a massive 24x24x24 area without absorbing water or turning wet.
* **Creative Lightning Rod:** Summons lightning at your crosshair on right-click, or on any entity hit. Must be crouch-placed.
* **Creative Bonemeal:** Guaranteed growth, never consumes item charges on failed attempts.
* **Creative Shulker Box:** Supports nested shulker boxes inside itself.
* **Creative Compass:** Can save and track any target coordinate directly without a Lodestone.
* **Creative Lodestone:** Right-clicking it (or using a linked compass) teleports the player directly above its location.
* **Creative Trial Key:** Unlocks any vault infinitely.
* **Creative Lead:** Leashes multiple entities simultaneously, including players and non-leadable mobs, also never snaps.
* **Creative Armor Trim:** Completely covers armor textures instead of just adding accent lines.

### Functional Blocks and Station Alternatives

* **Creative Furnace:** Instantly smelts items without requiring fuel.
* **Creative Anvil:** Free item repair, using books on any item, renaming, and combining without level caps or experience costs in both Survival and Creative mode. Never takes damage or breaks itself.
* **Creative Enchanting Table:** Applies maximum-level enchantments for free.
* **Creative Crafting:** Synthesizes recipes without consuming the ingredient items.
* **Creative Beacon:** Grants all positive status effects simultaneously (requires a base made entirely of Creative Ore blocks).
* **Creative Ore** *(Deepslate / Stone / Raw Item / Ingot Item / Raw Block / Compressed Block)***:** Decorative block required to activate the Creative Beacon. Can also craft creative tools from netherite using a Creative Smithing Template. Does not generate naturally in worldgen.
* **Creative Smithing Template:** Can be used to upgrade netherite tools to creative tools.
* **Creative Farmland:** Instantly grows planted crops to full maturity.
* **Creative Torch:** Emits light level 15 and can be waterlogged without breaking.
* **Creative Dripstone:** Instantly drips fluid, deals extreme damage when fallen on or dropped from above.
* **Creative Ice:** Zero surface friction.
* **Creative Honey:** Prevents jumping and completely halts entity movement upon contact.
* **Creative Soul Sand:** Reduces movement speed to zero (unaffected by Soul Speed).
* **Creative Cactus:** Deals infinite damage upon contact.
* **Creative Jukebox:** Doubles audio playback range and loops tracks automatically.
* **Creative Music Disc:** Opens an interface to select and play any sound effect in the game.
* **Creative Waxed Weathered Cut Copper Stairs:** Joke :P
* **Creative Hopper:** Moves entire inventories at a time. Doesn’t even have its own storage because of how fast it is.
* **Creative Redstone:** Has only an on and off state, never loses power the longer it goes.
* **Creative Comparitor:** Can be set to always produce a specific redstone power level whenever something powers it.
* **Creative Redstone Lamp:** Each power level corresponds to a dye color, with 0 being black.

### Storage and Containers

* **Creative Chest:** Infinite item storage capacity.
* **Creative Barrel:** Infinite storage, items placed inside are duplicate-dispensed without being consumed.
* **Creative Dispenser:** Dispenses items continuously without consuming contents.
* **Creative Item Frame:** Turns invisible when holding an item, contents can only be modified by Creative Mode players.

### Consumables and Throwable Items

* **Creative Bucket:** Infinite liquid source bucket (never empties). Ideally would auto-generate for any mods that add liquids.
* **Creative Snowball:** Inflicts 1 heart of damage on hit and is never consumed.
* **Creative Cookie:** Eaten once to permanently max out Food and Saturation meters.
* **Creative Apple:** Grants every positive status effect at its highest natural potency for 5 minutes.
* **Enchanted Creative Apple:** Grants every positive effect permanently and is not consumed on use.
* **Creative Pufferfish:** Instantly kills the player when eaten.
* **Creative Potion:** Grants Creative Flight mode for the duration of the effect.
* **Creative Ender Pearl:** Teleports without dealing fall damage or consuming the pearl.
* **Creative Firework:** Lasts indefinitely for infinite Elytra boosting.
* **Creative Goat Horn:** Opens a GUI menu to play any game sound, defaults to a [loud airhorn audio file.](https://www.myinstants.com/en/instant/very-loud-airhorn/)
* **Creative Milk:** Cures only every neutral and negative effect.
* **Creative Honey:** Cures every negative effect.

### Explosives, Fire and Fluids

* **Creative TNT:** Clears entire chunks down to/through Bedrock upon detonation.
* **Creative Fire Items** *(Flint and Steel / Fire Charge)*: Spreads quickly and inflicts 5× standard fire damage.
* **Creative Fire Effect:** Custom status effect used to handle Creative Fire logic.
* **Creative Lava:** Infinite burn duration, burns through Netherite, and inflicts Creative Fire on contact.
* **Creative Fluid:** A water-like fluid that heals friendly mobs/players while damaging hostiles.
* **Creative Coal:** Infinite fuel burn time.

### Entities, Vehicles and Structures

* **Creative Horse:** Equippable with wings to allow player-controlled flight.
* **Creative Saddle:** Right-click to instantly tame and ride almost any mob.
* **Creative Minecart:** Extremely fast, invulnerable to everything except Creative-mode players.
* **Creative Boat:** Carries up to 8 passengers, travels at extreme speeds, and is invulnerable to non-creative damage.
* **Creative Scaffolding:** Places without structural distance limits and never falls (can still be chain-broken).
* **Creative Dirt:** Does nothing lol.
* **Creative Clock:** Allows direct time-of-day adjustment via scrolling, which changes the texture and prints an action bar message of the time selected. Right clicking will apply that time.
* **Creative Sapling:** Grows into a custom Creative Wood and Leaves set. The tree is also HUGE and resembles spruce trees because those are clearly the best wood type.
* **Creative Creature:** Invincible, passive decorative mob. Can be tamed and bred too\!
* **Creative Mob:** Invincible, aggressive humanoid mob using the player model (like zombies). Will take any item a creative player right clicks them with. Can use just about any item (crossbows, melee, bows, tridents, even maces)

### Miscellaneous

* **Debug Stick Texture:** New unique texture for the debug stick, also removes the enchantment glint.
